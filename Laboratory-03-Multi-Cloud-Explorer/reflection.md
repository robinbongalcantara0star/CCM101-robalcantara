# Mission Reflection

Exploring the major cloud platforms during this mission provided a comprehensive overview of modern multi-cloud architectures. Among AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform, GCP impressed me the most because of its clean console interface, open-source friendliness, and unmatched leadership in Kubernetes and AI technologies. 

Despite their competition, key similarities exist across all three public cloud providers. They all deliver core cloud primitives—compute, storage, virtual networking, and identity management—under pay-as-you-go models. Each platform relies on structured global infrastructure consisting of regions and availability zones to deliver high availability and redundancy.

Through evaluating the client scenarios, I realized that business requirements strongly dictate cloud platform selection. An organization's decision is rarely purely technical; it is heavily influenced by existing infrastructure investments, internal team skills, budget limits, and specific workload needs like AI or Microsoft ecosystem dependencies.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that effective cloud architecture is about choosing the best tool for the specific business problem rather than defaulting to the most popular platform. Finally, updating my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio with structured Markdown files and evidence has reinforced my technical documentation habits and created a clear record of my learning progress.
