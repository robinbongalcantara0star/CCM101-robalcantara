# Checkpoint 8 – Mission Reflection

1. Which cloud platform impressed you the most?
Google Cloud Platform (GCP) impressed me the most due to its native focus on modern containerization and artificial intelligence. Its leadership in developing Kubernetes and seamlessly integrating specialized infrastructure like Tensor Processing Units (TPUs) via Vertex AI makes it stand out as an ideal platform for innovative, data-driven applications and cutting-edge software development.

 2. What similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP?
Despite their branding differences, all three platforms share fundamental core cloud architecture patterns. Each offers equivalent infrastructure services across compute virtual machines, object storage, relational and NoSQL databases, serverless functions, identity and access management (IAM), and virtual private networking. They all operate on global infrastructure models with availability zones and follow pay-as-you-go pricing structures.

 3. How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection?
Business requirements are the ultimate deciding factor in platform selection rather than technical features alone. Factors such as existing software ecosystems (e.g., heavy reliance on Microsoft Active Directory favoring Azure), industry compliance standards, budget constraints, internal team expertise, and specific performance needs (like low latency or specialized AI hardware) dictate which provider delivers the highest return on investment.

 4. What did you learn from acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant?
Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that cloud computing is not a "one-size-fits-all" solution. I learned how to analyze diverse organizational profiles—from agile startups to global enterprise environments—and map their unique business challenges directly to appropriate multi-cloud architectures and trade-offs.

 5. How has your GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio improved after completing this mission?
Completing this mission significantly enhanced my GitHub portfolio by transforming it into a practical demonstration of multi-cloud competency. Documenting hands-on investigations, comparative analysis matrices, service mapping tables, and client decision frameworks provides clear, concrete evidence of my ability to evaluate, architect, and communicate enterprise cloud solutions effectively.
